1. Introduction

Jabbrix Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Muster mobile application ("Muster", "the App") available on iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information weollect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

By downloading or using Muster you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Muster stores all user-created content exclusively on your device. This includes:

  • Alarm labels, scheduled times, repeat days, and configuration settings
  • Habit alarm types and schedules (e.g. hydration, stretch, meditation)
  • Location alarm names, saved coordinates, and trigger-radius settings
  • Reminder titles and scheduled times

None of this content is transmitted to our servers or any third party. It is held in encrypted local storage (MMKV) on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2.2 Location Data

When you create a Location Alarm, the App requests access to your precise device location. On Android, background location access is also requested so the alarm can fire when the App is not in the foreground. Location data is used solely to determine when you are within the radius you configured for that alarm. We do not store, transmit, or share your location with any server or third party. Location coordinates you enter are saved on-device only and are removed when you delete the corresponding alarm or uninstall the App.

2.3 Notification Permissions

The App requests permission to send local notifications (alarms) to your device. These notifications are scheduled locally and do not require an internet connection. No notification content is transmitted off your device.

2.4 In-App Purchases

Muster offers optional premium features through in-app purchases managed by RevenueCat, Inc. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat processes payment information through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We receive only anonymised purchase receipts and subscription-status tokens — we never see your payment card details. RevenueCat's privacy practices are governed by its own Privacy Policy (revenuecat.com/privacy).

RevenueCat may collect the follong on our behalf:

  • A RevenueCat-generated anonymous user ID
  • Purchase history and subscription status
  • Device platform (iOS / Android) and app version

2.5 Push Notifications (Optional — OneSignal)

The App includes optional integration with OneSignal for push notifications (e.g. motivational messages or feature announcements). This feature is disabled by default and will only be active if you explicitly opt in. If enabled, OneSignal may collect a push-notification token and basic device metadata. OneSignal's privacy practices are governed by its own Privacy Policy (onesignal.com/privacy).

2.6 Automatically Collected Technical Data

We do not operate analytics or crash-reporting services at this time. The App does not send any telemetry, usage events, or diagnostic data to our servers. Standard operating-system logs may be generated locally on your device but are not collected by us.

2.7 Accessibility Service (Android, Optional — "Prevent Power Off")

Muster offers an optional "Prevent Power Off" featthat uses Android's Accessibility Service API. When you turn it on, the service detects when the power menu opens while an alarm is ringing and closes it, so the alarm cannot be silenced by powering off the device. The service is only active while an alarm is ringing. It does not read, collect, store, or transmit any screen content, text you type, or other personal data, and nothing it observes leaves your device. This feature is off by default, requires your explicit consent, and can be disabled at any time in the App or in your device's Accessibility settings.

2.8 Health Data (Optional — Apple Health / Health Connect)

If you enable Sleep Insights, Muster requests read-only access to sleep data (sleep sessions and sleep stages) from Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android. This data is used only to display sleep summaries within the App. It is processed on your device, is never transmitted to our servers or any third party, and is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining. Muster do not write any data to Apple Health or Health Connect. Muster's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app (iOS) or Health Connect settings (Android).

2.9 Calendar Data (Optional)

If you enable the morning summary, Muster requests read-only access to your device calendar to show your upcoming events for the day after an alarm is dismissed. Calendar data is read on your device only, is not stored beyond what is needed to display it, and is never transmitted or shared. You can revoke access at any time in your device Settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above solely to:

  • Deliver alarm, habit, and location-based notification features you configure
  • Process and restore your in-app purchases via RevenueCat
  • Send optional push notifications if you have opted in via OneSignal
  • Respond to support requests you initiate by contacting us

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the App as described.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as follows:

  • RevenueCat — for in-app purchase processing and subscription management
  • OneSignal — only if you opt in to push notifications
  • Apple / Google — platform stores process payment information for purchases
  • Legal obligations — if required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety

Alarm data, habit data, location alarm coordinates, reminder content, health data, and calendar data are never shared with any third party.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All on-device data (alarms, habits, locations, reminders) is retained locally until you delete it within the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data from your device.

Purchase history held by RevenueCat is retained in accordance with RevenueCat's data-retention policy. You may requestn of your RevenueCat-associated records by contacting us at the email address below.

6. Device Permissions

Muster requests the following device permissions:

  • Precise Location (Foreground): Required to detect arrival at a saved location and trigger a Location Alarm.
  • Location in Background (Android): Required on Android so Location Alarms can fire when the App is not in the foreground.
  • Notifications: Required to display alarm and reminder notifications.
  • Alarms (iOS, AlarmKit): Required to schedule alarms that ring reliably, including in Silent mode and Focus.
  • Schedule Exact Alarms (Android): Required to fire alarms at the precise time you set.
  • Receive Boot Completed (Android): Required to re-register alarms after the device restarts so your schedule is not lost.
  • Wake Lock (Android): Required to keep the CPU awake long enough to sound the alarm reliably.
  • Full Screen Intent (Android): Required to display the alarm screen even when the device is locked.
  • Foreground Service (Android): Required to run the alarm-delivery service reliably in the background.
  • Accessibility Service (Android, optional): Used only for the opt-in "Prevent Power Off" feature, as described in Section 2.7.
  • Health Data — Read Sleep (optional): Used only for Sleep Insights, as described in Section 2.8.
  • Calendar — Read (optional): Used only for the morning summary, as described in Section 2.9.

You can revoke any permission at any time through your device Settings. Revoking a permission will disable the features that depend on it.

7. Children's Privacy

Muster is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Security

All data you create is stored in MMKV encrypted local storage on your device. We do not maintain servers that hold your personal data, which inherently limits exposure to server-side data breaches. You are responsible maintaining the security of your own device (e.g. screen lock, device encryption).

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Because Muster stores all user content on-device, you can exercise these rights directly within the App (delete individual alarms, clear all data, or uninstall the App). For purchase records held by RevenueCat or push-notification records held by OneSignal, please contact us using the details below and we will facilitate your request.

Residents of California (CCPA), the European Union / United Kingdom (GDPR/UK GDPR), or other jurisdictions with specific privacy laws may have additional rights. Please contact us to exercise any such rights.

10. Third-Party Services

This policy does not cover third-party services accessed through the App. Please review the privacy policies of RevenueCat and OneSignal independently. Links to their policies are provided in Section 2.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document and, for material changes, notify you via an in-app notice. Continued use of Muster after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
