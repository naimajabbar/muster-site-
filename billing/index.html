Subscriptions & Billing

One price, shown once

Muster Pro is offered at a fair price — no tricks, no dark patterns, no forced trials. Current prices are displayed in the app and on the App Store / Google Play listing.

Muster Pro plans

Choose the option that suits you. All plans unlock the same Pro features.

Monthly

Monthly

Auto-renewing monthly subscription. Renews each month until cancelled. Cancel anytime before the next renewal date.

Yearly

Yearly

Auto-renewing annual subscription. Renews each year until cancelled. Best value if you use Muster regularly. Cancel anytime before the renewal date.

Lifetime

Lifetime

A one-time purchase — pay once, own Muster Pro forever. No renewal, no recurring charge. The price is shown once, in the app.

No free trial

Muster Pro does not offer a free trial. You can evaluate Muster's core alarm features before purchasing. The current price for each plan is shown in the app and in the App Store / Google Play listing.

How billing works

All payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) via our payment provider RevenueCat. Jabbrix Studio never sees your payment card details.

Subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly) auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The renewal charge is applied to the Apple ID or Google account used at the time of purchase.

The Lifetime plan is a single charge with no future renewals.

Cancel on iPhone (iOS / App Store)

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside Muster. To cancel:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.

  2. Tap your name at the top of the screen.

  3. Tap Subscriptions.

  4. Find and tap Muster.

  5. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

You'll continue to have access to Pro features until the end of the current paid period. Cancelling does not issue a refund — see the refund section below.

Alternatively, go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions on any device.

Cancel on Android (Google Play)

To cancel a Muster subscription on Android:

  1. Open the Google Play app.

  2. Tap your profile icon in the top-right corner.

  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

  4. Find and tap Muster.

  5. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the prompts.

Access to Pro features continues until the end of the paid period.

Restore purchases

If you reinstall Muster or switch devices and your Pro status isn't showing, restore your purchase:

  1. Open Muster and navigate to Settings.

  2. Tap Restore purchases.

  3. Muster will contact Apple / Google to verify your entitlements. This may take a few seconds.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account used when you originally purchased.

If restoring doesn't work, contact support with your purchase receipt or order number.

Request a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by Jabbrix Studio.

Apple (iOS)

Request a refund through Apple's official tool:

reportaproblem.apple.com

Sign in with your Apple ID, find the Muster purchase, and select the reason for your refund request. Apple reviews and processes refund requests within a few days.

Google Play (Android)

Visit the Google Play refund policy page or use the Google Play app:

  1. Open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Order history.

  2. Find the Muster purchase and tap it.

  3. Tap Request a refund if the option is available (typically within 48 hours of purchase).

For purchases beyond Google Play's self-service window, contact Google Play support directly.

Switching plans

To switch between Monthly and Yearly, cancel your current subscription before the next renewal date, then purchase the other plan. Your Pro access continues until the end of the paid period.

To upgrade from any subscription to Lifetime, cancel your current subscription and then purchase the Lifetime plan from within the app. You won't be double-charged — the Lifetime purchase will activate once your current subscription period ends.

If you have questions about billing, contact us and we'll help.
